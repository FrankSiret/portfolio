# 3. “Engineering DNA”

Esta sería una sección diferencial.

Algo como:

## **How I think**

Y 6 principios.

### **Complexity**

I enjoy problems where the obvious solution isn't good enough.

### **Performance**

Optimize where it matters: CPU, memory, I/O, latency and infrastructure cost.

### **Simplicity**

Clean code isn't about fewer lines. It's about fewer reasons for things to break.

### **Adaptability**

Different domains, clients, teams and constraints require different engineering decisions.

### **Ownership**

I don't just implement designs. I challenge them, improve them and take responsibility for the outcome.

### **Pressure**

Production incidents, hard deadlines and ambiguous requirements are part of engineering.

Esto comunica mucho mejor el seniority que decir simplemente:

> Clean code, SOLID, Agile, Scrum.
